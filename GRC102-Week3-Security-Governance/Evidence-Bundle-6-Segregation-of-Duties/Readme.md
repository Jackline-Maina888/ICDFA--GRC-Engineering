
# Evidence Bundle 6 – Segregation of Duties Register

## Objective
To identify segregation of duties conflicts and recommend controls that reduce governance, fraud, and operational risks.

## Evidence Included
- Segregation of Duties Register
- Decision Recording and Assurance Note

## Key Outcomes
- Identified governance conflicts and risk areas.
- Recommended compensating controls.
- Established accountability and decision-recording requirements.
- Improved governance transparency and auditability.
